# Hardware History

## System

- Starlink Gen-2 router.
- Rectangular dish reporting hardware `rev3_proto2`.
- Dish cable replaced with a new cable before the September 2026 recovery investigation.
- Both cable terminals (router side and dish side) were physically rechecked and appeared good.

## Extended offline period

The Starlink system was left powered off for approximately one year before being returned to service in September 2026. On return, the dish was far behind the router in firmware and entered an extreme reboot loop before eventually recovering through a firmware installation.

## Physical accident

At an earlier time, the dish was accidentally driven over by a pickup truck. The external enclosure did not visibly break, but the user believes the dish flexed/bent internally. From that time onward, GPS reportedly never worked again.

This history is important when evaluating the persistent GPS failure, but it should not be used to explain unrelated symptoms without telemetry evidence.
